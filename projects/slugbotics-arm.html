Overview

A fully 3D-printable modular robotic arm with a preset joint-and-link system, allowing the number of degrees of freedom to be extended or retracted without significant physical redesign.

Subsystems

  • Control firmware (C): receives target joint angles from a host PC over UART. Independent per-joint PID loops on the onboard microcontroller drive each joint to its commanded position.
  • Electrical harness: JST/servo wiring routed through hollow joint shafts along the axis of rotation, minimizing cable flex and wear across repeated motion cycles.
  • Quick-release end effector mount: tool swaps without fasteners, compatible with any end effector adhering to the defined interface geometry.
