Overview

A biomimetic humanoid hand designed as a fully open-source module for attachment to the team's larger open-source humanoid platform, built around the Red Rabbit Robotics torso.

Design

  • Default-closed architecture: servo-driven mechanisms open the fingers against a spring return force.
  • Predictable maximum grip force: set by the spring, not the actuator.
  • Zero energy consumption while holding: the hand stays closed on a grasp with no servo load.
