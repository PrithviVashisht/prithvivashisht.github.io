Overview

A real-time control system that keeps a UR5e arm's end effector on a moving ball in CoppeliaSim. Validated on constant-velocity motion and on unpredictable, user-driven motion, with end-effector tracking within a 5 cm threshold.

Approach

  • Inverse kinematics: damped least-squares Jacobian IK, with exponential position filtering and per-joint step capping.
  • Null-space projection: pulls joints toward a neutral pose without disturbing end-effector accuracy.
  • Control architecture: joint servo delegated to CoppeliaSim's internal PD controller at 200 Hz substeps. A high-level computer computes targets and a low-level controller closes the loop, mirroring real-world systems.
